# Lore and geography

**November 24, 2026.** The owner retired the dragonkin premise. A replacement world story, its chronology and the end-game capstone remain open. The Sill is the current **Exploring** geography. Its place names, inhabitants and routes are available for concept work and playtest design. [Decision D-019](../design/decisions.md#d-019-world-premise)

## The Sill

Follow a load of grain west from Common Weir. It passes through the sluices, travels downriver through Brackwater Reach and lands under the cranes at Sedge Quay. Salt fish travel back upriver. East of the weir, packers cross Lantern Scarp to supply Ventfold, whose pipewrights send hardware back over the saddle. These journeys link the three regions even when every delver stays home. The [concept atlas](../../assets/concept-art/the-sill/README.md) identifies the proposed crossings and views.

| Region | Places on the route | What a traveler can learn |
|---|---|---|
| Tidemouth | Sedge Quay in Brackwater Reach, Shellbank Steps and Knellwater Polder | Water stains on the white steps mark the reach of the tide. In the polder the dry dike stays above the funeral road, where the drowned still carry their dead against the ebb. |
| Warded Basin | Common Weir in Weirfields, Lantern Scarp, the Fallen Vault and the Stridegrave | The cart road continues over the saddle past the Vault turn. A separate baggage ramp descends to the giants’ battlefield. At the Rain Shield the survivors draw clean water and wash their dead. |
| Cinder Uplands | Ventfold in Flue March, Pale Crown and Palmfall Caldera | A traveler at the Keel can inspect tracks from the wind shelf. At Palmfall the ground still erupts along the furrows of a god’s handprint. Hoodbacks guard clutches on higher ledges. The god’s identity and the age of the strike remain open. |

## Adventurers and delving

**Provisional.** Delving and warding are trades. Someone pays to keep a route open, recover a missing load or bring home a legible record of an older settlement. In the Fallen Vault, copying the relief preserves the surface the salvage foreman intends to strip. A delver has a reason to learn the patrol and the way out. The archivist has a reason to remember who brought the rubbing. [First playable](../../tasks/nightcap-systems/spec.md)

## Ruins, inhabitants and beliefs

Older masonry survives where people have found a use for it. A last-cycle lintel marks the Vault entrance, and a worn relief outlasts the people who carved it. “Last-cycle” identifies a prior era. Its date, the fate of its builders and the relationship between distant ruins are open.

Local work and obligations give inhabitants interests of their own. Taking a fitting from a grave at the Stridegrave means something different to a surviving giant than buying repair iron at Ventfold. Living creatures also have local needs. The eels shelter behind the Bell Sill, and nesting hoodbacks feed near a cold seep in Palmfall. The number, origins, religions and languages of the peoples of the Sill remain open.
