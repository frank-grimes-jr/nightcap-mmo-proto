# Image-Generation Prompt Pack — GitHub Pages Explainer

**Exploring · revised November 24, 2026.** This pack supersedes the August world, fauna and end-game prompts. For an active world-art reference use [the Sill atlas](../../assets/concept-art/the-sill/README.md). The [setting record](../lore/structure.md) leaves the overarching story open.

## How to use this file

Image models do **not** remember context between images. So for **every** image:

1. Paste **Section 1 (Style Preamble)** first — verbatim.
2. Then paste the specific numbered prompt from **Section 2**.
3. Then paste **Section 3 (Negative / Do-Not)** at the end.

Generate one image per numbered prompt. Keep the same preamble every time so the whole set shares one visual identity.

**Honesty note (put this on the website, not in the prompt):** these are aspirational *concept* images. The shipping game targets low-spec / integrated-GPU hardware, so in-engine fidelity will be lower than this concept art. Do not present these as gameplay screenshots.

## Section 1 — Style Preamble (paste before every prompt)

> **Art direction.** Give near and far objects distinct silhouettes. Use grounded proportions, hand-painted materials, atmospheric depth and natural light. Keep a single working landmark legible at thumbnail size. Limit each scene to an earthy base and one or two local accents. Treat this as concept illustration.
>
> **Setting.** The Sill’s river joins a western salt port, a grain basin and eastern headwaters. Workers carry freight, maintain crossings and salvage stone from older settlements. Name the place, who uses it, the route into the scene and one local condition that changes how the route feels underfoot.
>
> **Originality.** Use original designs for all architecture, clothing, creatures, symbols and tools. Take geography and material landmarks from the atlas plate for the named place.
>
> **Finish:** cinematic concept-art lighting, cohesive color grade, no on-image text, no logos, no watermarks, no UI/HUD.

## Section 2 — Image Prompts

### A. World, tone, and zones

**1. Hero banner · the cart road to Lantern Scarp** *(21:9 or 16:9)*

Stand on the east road above Common Weir beside a packer tightening a load of grain. Behind the packer, the green exchange roof sits above three sluice arches. Ahead, the road climbs to a saddle. A smaller path branches beneath the Scarp’s broken lintel. Give the cart enough room to continue past the turn.

**2. The working river** *(16:9)*

Survey the river west toward the tidal flats. In the near fields, grain carts descend to the water. Farther along, reed beds widen and the twin lifting frames at Sedge Quay rise above the landing. Bring a salt-fish barge upriver against the grain traffic. Show what connects these places in the shape of the waterway.

**3. The common fire** *(16:9 or 3:2)*

View Common Weir’s hearth court from the open east gate. Delvers share the fire while a carter takes a worn flue collar to the workshop. Use warm firelight, the green exchange roof and practical repairs in the older stonework to make the court recognizable on a return visit.

**4. The funeral stair at Knellwater** *(16:9)*

Stand at the top of the pallbearers’ stair below the roofless bell gable. Tide lines stripe the masonry. As the lower steps emerge, the drowned bear their dead against the ebb. Keep the dry dike crest visible so the viewer can choose a route.

**5. The Fallen Vault** *(16:9 or 4:5)*

Look round the sight-blocking corner in the Bloom. The front edge of the wall remains in frame, a pale shelf of fungus catches the light, and a patrol passes across the far gallery. Keep enough of the stairs behind the observer visible to read the exit.

### B. Two field studies

**6. A nursery in Palmfall** *(4:3)*

Show a hoodback sheltering its clutch on a warm ledge above a cold seep, with beetles at the waterline. Give the animal a place to feed and somewhere to withdraw. Include the abandoned hoist farther downslope so a delver can see a way around the nursery. Use the Palmfall brood-shelf plate for continuity.

**7. The Rain Shield** *(16:9)*

Stand beneath the rim of the giant shield at the Stridegrave. The concave shield catches rainwater and the survivors use it to wash their dead. A human-sized broken cart underneath establishes scale. Frame the high baggage ramp beyond the bearers so a traveler can recognize a quiet way past the burial party.

### C. The six delver classes (original armor & designs; grounded proportions)

For each: a full-body character concept, three-quarter view, neutral or lightly-environmental backdrop, with a small readable prop/pose that sells the fantasy. All armor and gear **original**. Keep proportions grounded. Give each the noted accent color from its resource identity (HP = deep red/life, MP = arcane blue-white, Energy = warm amber). *(aspect 3:4 portrait each)*

**8. The Holder** — mitigation tank; **heavy plate armor**; one-handed weapon and a **large steel shield**; amber (Energy) accents. A disciplined, immovable frontier bulwark planted in a defensive stance in a ruin doorway. Silhouette: unmovable wall. Hook: *"the door that won't open."*

**9. The Reaver** — lifesteal tank; **heavy armor**, more brutal and worn than the Holder's; a **great two-handed weapon**; amber-and-blood-red (Energy + HP) accents, faint life-draining motif on the blade. Aggressive, relentless, mid-lunge. Hook: *"the wound that heals by dealing wounds."*

**10. The Warder** — shields tank / spell-tank; **heavy armor**; a one-handed melee weapon in the main hand and, in the **off-hand, a SUMMONED SHIELD OF LIGHT** — a glowing conjured ward, clearly arcane energy rather than physical steel; cool arcane blue-white (MP) glow. A recovered last-cycle survival art. Solemn, protective. Hook: *"the light carried through the last dark."*

**11. The Mender** — reactive healer; **medium armor** (mobile, practical, works close to danger — a frontier field-physician, NOT a robed cleric hiding at the back); an **implement/focus** plus a **one-handed sidearm**; restorative warm-gold light with MP-blue undertone; a field kit or satchel of salves. Steady, competent, unglamorous. Hook: *"the reason you walked back out."*

**12. The Binder** — The published Bloom portrait was rendered with the built-in image generator using this prompt.

> Stylized-concept. Vertical 3:4 full-body original MMORPG class concept painting in the grounded, grim, low-saturation Nightcap visual language. One lightly armored human delver, the Binder, stands poised at a sharp right-angle corner of grey masonry in the Fallen Vault’s pale Bloom gallery. Worn travel leathers, charcoal wool coat, scuffed boots, small bone bobbins and spools at belt. Their extended fingers control three taut blue-white luminous threads stretched crosswise in front of a boot of a distant adult human salvage scout just rounding the corner. The Binder watches the scout, choosing the right moment to stall pursuit. Clear class silhouette, a little pale shelf fungus and cool overcast light on damp stone. No animals, dragons, wings, scales, heraldic motifs, ornate magic glyphs, lettering, title, UI or grids. Dark grounded painterly brushed gouache and oil, convincing anatomy, clear in portrait card crop. Original restrained classic-fantasy RPG character illustration.

**13. The Kindler** — burst damage / arcane striker; **light armor**; an **arcane implement/focus** channeling a front-loaded destructive spike — a recovered last-cycle destructive art (ember-like energy, but not necessarily literal fire; an original arcane look). Cool MP-blue channel resolving into a bright volatile point. Glass-cannon: dangerous, exposed, mid-cast. Hook: *"the last-cycle fire, briefly rekindled."*

### D. Optional / explanatory (nice-to-have)

**14. Three-class identity diagram illustration** *(16:9)*
One delver rendered so they *read clearly as one primary class* (e.g., a Holder) but carry subtle accents of a secondary and tertiary — a faint Warder-light ward and a Kindler ember at the fingertips — visualizing the primary-dominant, three-class combination. Grounded, single figure, elegant.

**15. Knowledge-as-progression / the codex** *(3:2)*
An intimate scene: a delver by lantern-light annotating a personal journal/codex with a rubbing taken from a ruin relief and a sketch of a mason’s tool mark that matches one on the rubbing — the "earned knowledge" that sharpens from vague rumor toward exact source. Scholarly, warm, quiet; contrast to the danger outside.

## Section 3 — Negative / Do-Not (paste after every prompt)

> Avoid: any recognizable characters, armor, creatures, races, architecture, symbols, logos, or UI from World of Warcraft, EverQuest, or any existing game; cartoon/chibi exaggeration or overly cute proportions; photorealistic AAA/ESO-style rendering; sci-fi, modern, or steampunk elements; on-image text, captions, watermarks, signatures, or UI/HUD overlays; cluttered or busy compositions; neon or candy color palettes.

## Notes for whoever runs these

- Generate the six class portraits **in one session** if the tool supports style continuity, so armor language and rendering match across the roster.
- If a tool allows a seed/style reference, lock it after image 1 (the hero) to carry the grade through the set.
- Names in this pack (Holder, Reaver, Warder, Mender, Binder, Kindler) are **working descriptors**, not final IP names (D-008). Do not render these words into the images.
