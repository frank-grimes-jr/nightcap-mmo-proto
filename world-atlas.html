The Sill

One river connects the three regions of this proposed continent. Start at the hearth in Common Weir, then follow the roads out into the Sill. Follow it from the continental map down to a delver’s view on foot.

Tidemouth

The river meets the sea among working salt docks, stranded shell terraces, and burial fields drowned by the tide.

Brackwater ReachSedge Quay

Read this coast by elevation. Loaded barges stop below the warehouse ridge at Sedge Quay. When water covers the lower treads, porters can still take the raised towpath east toward Weirfields.

Shellbank StepsThe Stranded Ring

Shell grit gives way under the low shelf. The raised path keeps its footing and winds behind the last-cycle mooring ring. Both approaches reach the abandoned watch foundation, where a packer can see the estuary below.

Knellwater PolderThe Bell Sill

A sea wall failed around the burial fields below Shellbank Steps. At ebb the drainage ditches empty seaward. A file of drowned pallbearers walks the old funeral road the other way, still trying to carry a litter to the bell.

Warded Basin

Grain roads meet at a three-arched weir. Beyond the fields, the scarp shelters the Fallen Vault and overlooks the graves of giants.

WeirfieldsCommon Weir

The exchange sits where the river narrows enough for three sluice arches. Its yard draws the west towpath, the northern levee and the east delvers’ road to a common starting point.

Lantern ScarpThe Threshold

From the fields, the road rises past a sheltered bind camp. A patrol can cross the exposed bend above it. One spur ends at the Fallen Vault’s public Threshold. The main cart road keeps climbing toward the eastern saddle.

The Fallen VaultThe Bloom turn

A public Threshold gives way to the pale upper Bloom. Down in the Emberways a short fissure crossing competes with a longer dry gallery. Both lead to the Vault relief. A separate egress leads back toward daylight.

The StridegraveThe Rain Shield

A charge of giants drove the road into the earth below Lantern Scarp. Freight wagons still lie flattened in its ruts. One fallen shield catches a pond of rainwater. Surviving shield-bearers carry their dead there to wash and bury them.

Cinder Uplands

Follow the heatworks up to the wind-scoured Crown, or take the abandoned kiln road toward the fires of Palmfall.

Flue MarchVentfold

Old vents open at the feet of stepped basalt. Ventfold concentrates the heat in clay-lined channels, then carts fittings back over the saddle. The road climbs above the pipes before it turns north toward the Crown.

Pale CrownThe Keel

Pale mineral crust coats the high plateau beneath its stone keel. A sheltered fold lets a lone surveyor approach the open shelf and withdraw after reading the tracks. Meltwater collects in the deepest prints.

Palmfall CalderaThe Last Hoist

A god pressed its hand into the mountain beyond Flue March. Four long gouges and a thumb cut across the old kiln district. An eruption has filled the palm with fire. Lava follows the lowest fingers past the roofs of buried workshops.
