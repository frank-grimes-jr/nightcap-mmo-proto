01 The premise

A continent built
along a river.

The river starts in the Pale Crown and reaches the sea at Sedge Quay. Grain travels west from Common Weir. Salt fish come back inland. The pipewrights of Ventfold need both to keep their crews fed.

Delvers travel these same roads. An archivist wants a rubbing from the Fallen Vault. A barge crew needs to know which landing clears at ebb. Trace the road through the Warded Basin.

Exploring geography. The Sill is one proposed continent. Its inhabitants know the histories of individual places. The larger story connecting them remains open.

02 The frontier

Every mile out
changes the rules.

At Shellbank Steps the old mooring ring stands above dry ground. Follow the breached sea wall down to Knellwater and the path disappears at high tide. One coastline asks two different things of a traveler.

03 The last cycle

Once a living place.
Now a tomb of knowledge.

The town archivist wants a copy of the relief. A salvage foreman wants the same stone. A delver who gets close enough to take a rubbing has something worth bringing home. Enter the Fallen Vault.

04 The outer roads

Who holds
the crossing?

At Knellwater Polder, drowned bearers carry a litter against the ebb. A living keeper waits on the dike for the last of them to pass. The old funeral road is still in use.

In Palmfall Caldera, lava runs through the furrows of a god’s handprint. Hoodbacks shelter their eggs on warm tuff above the flow. A traveler can reach the abandoned hoist by taking the rim path around the brood.

05 The delvers

Six ways to
walk back out.

Classes grew out of frontier work. A Holder can keep an enemy facing the shield while a Binder buys time at the corner. A solo delver can train another discipline at Common Weir and prepare for the same pull.

Holder, Reaver, Warder, Mender, Binder, and Kindler are working descriptors, not final names. The roster remains Exploring.

06 Character identity

One delver.
Three disciplines.

Your first discipline is permanent. The sanctuary trainer offers two more. A Holder preparing for the Bloom might learn a Binder’s control or a Mender’s heal before attempting the corner alone.

07 Earned knowledge

What you know
is part of your build.

The codex begins with rumor: a region, a creature, a fragment. As you encounter related foes, ruins, and evidence, uncertainty sharpens toward an exact source.

Routes, behaviors, safe places, dangerous times, and forgotten techniques are all progression. The next victory starts long before the next fight.

The intended loop

Venture out. Encounter danger.
Learn. Prepare. Return. Succeed.

Solo-capable does not mean safe. Shared-world does not mean mandatory groups. Mastery is the difference between possible and earned.
