# Product Vision

## Status

Provisional, 2026-08-02.

## How might we

How might we create an original MMORPG that restores danger, mystery, earned competence, memorable towns, and the feeling of inhabiting an unknown world while ensuring that every activity, including end-game dungeon encounters, can be completed by a single player?

## Experience thesis

The world does not know that the player is the protagonist.

Players begin vulnerable. The wilderness is not arranged for their convenience. Competence is earned through practice, knowledge, preparation, and failure. Towns feel like home because the world outside them is genuinely dangerous.

The intended emotional loop is:

`venture out -> encounter danger -> fail or barely escape -> learn -> prepare -> return -> succeed`

## Current pillars

### Danger creates immersion

Open-world enemies are not universally scaled into guaranteed fairness. Observation, route choice, preparation, retreat, and knowledge matter. Solo-capable means possible with sufficient mastery, not automatically safe.

### Competence is earned

Weapon, spell, trade, and utility skills improve through relevant use within level-based caps. Early inconsistency demonstrates growth, with protections against extreme bad-luck streaks.

### Knowledge is progression

Players learn routes, creature relationships, faction boundaries, NPC behavior, recipes, safe places, dangerous times, and environmental signals.

### Towns are sanctuaries

Settlements have geography, culture, services, factions, and emotional identity. Returning matters because leaving matters.

### Quests belong to the world

Quest guidance is approachable and closer to World of Warcraft than early EverQuest ambiguity, while leaving room for later discussion about the exact amount of map and objective assistance.

### Convenience is discovered

Travel becomes easier after the player physically explores a zone and discovers its hidden waypoint.

### Solo-capable does not mean solitary

All required content, including end-game encounters, must be completable by one player. Shared-world presence, cooperation, trade, and optional grouping may still matter.

## Product constraints

- Original intellectual property and original expression throughout.
- Fully 3D client with free first- and third-person camera zoom.
- Low-spec presentation comparable in technical simplicity to early polygonal MMORPGs, refined through consistent art direction.
- Authoritative server and persistent database connectivity.
- Local client and patcher.
- Modern, maintainable technology selected only after the product requirements are sufficiently clear.
- Visual and performance targets validated on representative integrated graphics hardware.
